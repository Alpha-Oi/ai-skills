# Biological basis and limits

Complementary DNA strands can provide templates for synthesis and some repair.
A mismatch alone does not identify the erroneous base: strand-directed repair needs
additional discrimination. See [DNA Replication Mechanisms](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26850/).

Complementarity is not literally RAID, a checksum, or a general error-correcting
code. Biological repair uses molecular machinery; recovery is neither instantaneous
nor guaranteed for every lesion. An engineering analogy does not demonstrate a
historical pathway or intentional design. The RNA-world hypothesis is not disproved
by comparing RNA with an unprotected file.

Engineering use: require an explicit consistency relation, a corruption model, and
independent grounds for choosing the repair template. Never infer that disagreement
between two representations always allows reconstruction of the original.
