# Biological basis and limits

Movement between separate organelle membranes differs from translocation between
the two leaflets of one bilayer. Flippases and floppases are commonly named for
opposite directions of lipid translocation; scramblases facilitate bidirectional
redistribution and can dissipate asymmetry. Terminology and substrates depend on
the transporter and membrane context:
[Regulation of transbilayer plasma membrane phospholipid asymmetry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12576505/).

Lipid synthesis is not exclusively an ER process. The user's repeated dictation is
one conceptual input, not evidence for independent mechanisms. The skill does not
claim that scrambling maintains asymmetry or that transporters intentionally plan.

Engineering use: distinguish controlled directional transitions from separately
triggered redistribution. Biological mixing never implies permission to mix trusted
and untrusted data or override an access boundary.
