# Biological basis and limits

Glycan diversity includes linkage positions, stereochemistry, and branching.
Recognition may depend on arrangement rather than composition alone:
[Oligosaccharides and Polysaccharides](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK453048/).

The glycocalyx motivates an analogy to a changing recognition surface. It is not
more complex than its containing cell by an established universal factor. A fixed
nine-symbol alphabet, universal millisecond rebuilding, and a fixed ratio of
complexity to proteins are not assumed here. Combinatorial counts require a stated
alphabet and chemical constraints and do not measure realized biological complexity.

Engineering use: preserve context that affects interpretation and manage coherent
updates. Do not treat theoretical configuration counts as a reason to enlarge a
software data model. No claim about update rates transfers to software performance.
